# Provenance and licensing

The maintainer authorized this repository's source and original synthetic media
under MIT. This authorization applies only to the files distributed here.
Consumer applications, their documentation, artwork and data are outside scope.

The showcase and its PNG/GIF captures use this package's runnable Flutter
compositions and synthetic content. No personal records, photographs, audio or
real quotations are bundled.

The example uses unmodified Inter and Caveat fonts with their original SIL OFL
1.1 copyright and license notices in example/assets/fonts. These are example
assets, not an exported font dependency. FFmpeg is an optional external capture
tool; no binary or codec library is distributed. See dependencies.md.

The curve/ink primitives and drawing playground are authored here using Flutter
paths and synthetic waypoints. The drawing capture also renders the SDK-provided
Material Icons font; that font is loaded from the SDK for capture and is not
copied into this repository. No consumer artwork or screenshots are bundled.

Palette-independent pigment and glyph-shading logic is adapted from
maintainer-owned source, with explicit inputs, bounded work and native paragraph
configuration. This generic code is covered by the maintainer's MIT authorization.
The botanical specimens, balloon and motion comparisons are newly authored
synthetic examples. They do not reproduce a consumer application's artwork.

The cabinet sprite sheet is original synthetic artwork authored with Flutter in
tool/author_layer_asset.dart and distributed under MIT. Its runtime example
imports the resulting PNG unchanged. No third-party or consumer image is used.

Contour insets use clipper2 0.0.3 as a pub-resolved dependency under BSL-1.0;
its algorithm/source is not vendored or relicensed as MIT. The bounded Flutter
path adapter, seam specimens and updated synthetic card captures are authored
here. Dependency notices and inspected LICENSE hash are recorded separately.

Paper finish/edge algorithms and specimen captures are original synthetic work
authored here under MIT. Stock names describe visual composition recipes over
existing paper grain, not externally sourced material scans or product assets.

The 0.1.0 boundary correction removes the earlier consumer-specific kite-tail
source/export and the captures containing that motion. The example uses a new,
simple waypoint ribbon local to the example. This is a forward source cleanup,
not a claim that historical Git objects or prior immutable versions are erased.
New motion captures require review before replacing the removed documentation
media. Consumer artwork remains the consumer's responsibility.

Cord/ribbon/embroidered trim algorithms and the original looping cord, shaped
paper and leaf-sprig study are maintainer-authored geometry. No private reference
image, name, motif or consumer artwork is distributed. The trim image is captured
from example/lib/trim_studies.dart using the existing licensed fonts.

The material cover and relief-object sheet are original code-native compositions
in example/lib/cover_studies.dart, captured by tool/capture_cover.dart. Shell,
feather, crescent, landscape and sprig geometry are newly authored. They reuse
public material, watercolor, relief, trim and pose primitives, with existing
example-only licensed fonts. No private screenshot or consumer artwork is used.
